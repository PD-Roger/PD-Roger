## From Concept to Mass Production

A hardware product moves through multiple engineering phases before reaching mass production.

Each phase requires different technical decisions, validation activities, and collaboration between engineering, manufacturing, quality, and suppliers.

**Concept & Product Definition**

Define product requirements, technical feasibility, product architecture, and key development risks.

**Mechanical Design & Prototype Build**

Develop mechanical solutions, establish design requirements, optimize manufacturability, and validate key design concepts.

**EVT (Engineering Validation Test)**

Verify engineering performance, identify technical risks, and drive design improvements.

**DVT (Design Validation Test)**

Validate product design, reliability requirements, and readiness for manufacturing.

**PVT (Production Validation Test)**

Qualify manufacturing processes, tooling capability, production flow, and quality controls.

**Ramp Up & Mass Production**

Stabilize production, improve yield, manage quality, and support continuous improvement at volume.
